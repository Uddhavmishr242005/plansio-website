Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how PLANSIO (operated by J.K. Enterprises, Neemrana, Rajasthan) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and place orders with us. By using our website, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

1 Overview

We at PLANSIO are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy applies to all personal information collected through our website (plansio-website) and through our order process including calls, WhatsApp and email communications.

🌿 We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party under any circumstances.

2 Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you interact with us:

TypeWhat We CollectWhy
Order InformationName, phone, address, email, product, quantityTo process and deliver your order
Contact InformationName, phone, email, messageTo respond to your queries
Payment InformationPayment method (COD/UPI/Bank), transaction referenceTo confirm payment
Device InformationBrowser type, IP address (approximate)For website security and analytics

We do not collect sensitive information such as Aadhaar numbers, PAN, bank passwords or any biometric data.

3 How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling your product orders
  • Sending order confirmation and delivery updates via call or WhatsApp
  • Responding to customer support queries
  • Improving our products and services based on feedback
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring transaction security
  • Sending promotional offers (only if you have opted in)

We will never contact you for unsolicited marketing calls unless you have specifically requested updates from us.

4 Information Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following limited cases:

  • Delivery Partners: We share your name, phone and address with our courier/delivery partners solely for delivering your order.
  • Payment Processors: When paying via UPI or bank transfer, your payment information is handled by your bank or UPI app — we do not store card or UPI PIN details.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or court order.

⚠️ We will never share your data with advertisers, marketers or data brokers.

5 Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Order data submitted through our website is stored locally and accessible only to our authorized team members.

However, please note that no method of data transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6 Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses browser localStorage (similar to cookies) to store your order and preference data on your device. This data is used to:

  • Remember order details for the admin panel
  • Maintain your session during browsing

No tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies are used on this website. You can clear this data any time by clearing your browser's local storage.

7 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: You can ask us to correct any inaccurate information.
  • Right to Deletion: You can request that we delete your personal information from our records.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You can opt out of promotional communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at Plansio.Jk@gmail.com
