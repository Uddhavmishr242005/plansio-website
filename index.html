Usage Guide

How to Use PLANSIO

4 simple steps for the best results

1
🪴

Fill Pot with Soil

Prepare your pot or garden bed. Loosen the topsoil 2–3 inches deep for best absorption.

2
🌱

Add PLANSIO Vermicompost

Sprinkle 50–100g evenly around the plant roots. Avoid direct contact with stem or leaves.

3
🪛

Mix with Soil

Gently mix into the topsoil using a garden trowel for even distribution and better nutrient release.

4
💧

Water & Watch Growth

Water thoroughly after application. Repeat every 30–45 days during the growing season.

Best applied in early morning or evening. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Safe for all plants — vegetables, fruits, flowers and field crops.
