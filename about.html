Our Story

We Believe in the Power of Nature

PLANSIO was born from a simple belief — that the best way to grow healthy food is to first nurture healthy soil. We started our journey with a vision to bring affordable, high-quality organic fertilizers to every farmer and gardener across India.

Manufactured by J.K. Enterprises, a trusted name based in Neemrana, Rajasthan, PLANSIO Vermicompost is crafted from carefully selected organic materials through a controlled process that ensures consistent quality and maximum nutrient content.

Our products are used by home gardeners, farmers, nurseries and agricultural businesses across India. We are committed to making organic farming accessible, effective and economical for everyone.
