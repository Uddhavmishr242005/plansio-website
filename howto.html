📋 How to Use PLANSIO

Simple steps for best results with PLANSIO Vermicompost

1
🪴

Fill Pot with Soil

Prepare your pot or garden bed. Loosen the topsoil 2–3 inches deep for best absorption of nutrients.

2
🌱

Add PLANSIO Vermicompost

Sprinkle 50–100g evenly around the plant roots. Avoid direct contact with stem or leaves.

3
🪛

Mix with Soil

Gently mix into the topsoil using a garden trowel for even distribution and better nutrient release.

4
💧

Water & Watch Growth

Water thoroughly after application. Repeat every 30–45 days during the growing season.

💡 Pro Tips

Best Time to Apply

Apply in early morning or evening for best results. Avoid applying in direct harsh sunlight.

Always Water After

Water the plant immediately after application to activate nutrients and help soil absorption.

Storage

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Keep bag tightly sealed after use.

Use with Neem Powder

Mix your free Neem Powder into the soil for enhanced pest protection and better results.
